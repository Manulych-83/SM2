# Camp 1 artwork

Generated with the built-in image generator on 2026-09-13, then moved into this project. Separate presentation assets, not simulation or save data.

- `forest.png`: landscape environment derived from the approved `outputs/camp-concept-1/camp-v1.png` reference, removing UI, hero, fire, shelter and equipment. Painterly wooded valley at dusk, empty foreground for compositing.
- `places.png`: two-panel environment atlas. Left: mossy stone ruins. Right: technological enclave outskirts. Same camera/ground horizon and empty foreground; no UI, people or camp equipment.
- `fire.png`: isolated campfire, charred logs and stone ring, transparent alpha, painterly RPG style; no terrain or people.
- `stash.png`: isolated low weathered wooden chest with leather straps, transparent alpha, three-quarter view and warm lighting; no treasure glow or scenery.

Placement, atlas selection and menu presentation are defined in `content/presentation/camp_scene.json`. Fire is decorative; stash visibility comes from an actual local ground item. The existing character/equipment atlas remains in `assets/art1/`.
