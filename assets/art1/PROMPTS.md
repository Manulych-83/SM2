# Art 1 — исходные изображения

## ground-v1.png

Create a single square opaque texture atlas for an original hand-painted 2D tactical RPG set in overgrown ancient technological ruins. Exactly TWO columns and TWO rows of equal square texture samples, edge to edge, no gutters, no labels, no grid, no props, no shadows of objects, no perspective horizon. Top left: muted earthy olive grass with patches of bare soil and tiny moss, mostly fine low-contrast texture. Top right: dusty ochre-brown rough gravel and packed dirt, tiny stones. Bottom left: worn grey-brown ancient paving, small irregular cracked flat flagstones with thin moss joints and occasional very subtle dead turquoise traces, top-down surface. Bottom right: dark muted olive earth and low green moss. Hand-painted ink and gouache realism with restrained detail, mature tabletop art, warm-grey and desaturated olive palette, diffuse lighting, no shiny highlights, no large objects, no text, no hexes. All four squares are seamless material samples seen straight down, designed to be clipped into small hex tiles under readable game characters.

Созданы встроенным imagegen 12 сентября 2026. Авторские изображения для SM2; исходный атлас сохранён без растровой переработки. Нарезка выполняется прямоугольниками AtlasTexture в движке. Это первый набор, а не завершённый каталог графики.

## figures-v1.png

Create ONE production-ready transparent PNG sprite atlas for an original hand-painted 2D tactical RPG called SM2. Square canvas, precisely 4 equal columns by 4 equal rows, 16 distinct isolated sprites, one centered inside each equal square cell with generous transparent gutters. NO printed grid, no text, no labels, no numbers, no background, no ground shadows. Real alpha transparency. All art consistent: mature grounded ink-and-gouache illustrated tabletop miniatures, clean dark ink contour, muted ochre linen, worn russet leather, blue-grey steel, soft upper-left light, readable broad shapes, intricate but restrained hand-painted texture; not cartoon chibi, not pixel art, not 3D render. Slight elevated frontal three-quarter perspective facing camera-right. For bodies full body including feet, compact natural proportions, fixed standing ready pose. Atlas ordered strictly left to right top to bottom. Row 1: (1) complete adult male human base body, short brown hair, neutral ochre linen tunic belted at waist, dark trousers, leather boots, TWO natural arms with elbows bent slightly and empty hands at waist, full body head to toes, no weapon or shield; (2) complete broad hunched humanoid mutant in ragged cloth, rough green-grey skin, asymmetric shoulders, full body head to toes, unarmed, same camera; (3) isolated adult human head with dark short hair, same camera, no neck below collar; (4) isolated bald bearded adult human head same camera. Row 2: (1) isolated padded brown leather torso vest with open arm holes, no body, no arms, no head, no legs, shaped to overlay the human torso; (2) isolated blue-grey chainmail torso vest with leather straps, no body or arms, shaped to overlay same torso; (3) isolated simple open-faced steel helmet, empty, matching same head angle; (4) isolated articulated cybernetic RIGHT forearm and closed metal hand, vertical with hand down, weathered dark steel and a tiny turquoise insert, no organic body. Row 3: (1) isolated single upright straight sword blade up, pommel down; (2) isolated single upright wooden spear point up; (3) isolated single upright iron hand axe blade up; (4) isolated round wooden shield with iron rim seen three-quarter front. Row 4: (1) isolated simple wooden bow upright; (2) isolated broken ancient stone-and-metal pillar, three-quarter top view, moss and small dead turquoise inlay, broad base, no ground; (3) isolated low cluster of grey angular rocks with moss, top view; (4) isolated tuft of dry grass with a few small green leaves, top view. Keep every sprite fully inside its own cell, no overlaps, no detached debris between cells. Body sprites nearly fill their cell vertically, with all feet and heads intact. All remaining props individually centered and large inside their own cell. This is a usable game sprite sheet, not an illustrated mockup or screenshot.
